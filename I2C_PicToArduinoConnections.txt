In the Explorer 16/32:
- the Pin 58 is the I2C clock (SCL2/RA2)
- the Pin 59 is I2C data (SDA2/RA3)

Connections (using a breadboard):
- connect the ground of the Arduino to the ground of the Explorer 16/32
- connect the A4 (SDA, the I2C data) pin of the Arduino to the Pin 59 of the Explorer 16/32
- connect the A5 (SCL, the I2C clock) pin of the Arduino to the Pin 58 of the Explorer 16/32
- connect a pull-up resistor of 5kohm from the I2C data line to the 5V of the Arduino
- connect a pull-up resistor of 5kohm from the I2C clock line to the 5V of the Arduino
